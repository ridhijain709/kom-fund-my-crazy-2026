04 Answers to the 4 Hardest Government & Policy Queries

QUERY 01

"Municipal tender processes take 12–18 months. How can a startup deploy quickly?"

The Strategy: We completely bypass commercial bidding tenders during Phase 1 & 2. By leveraging the State Regulatory Sandbox provisions under the Startup India mission, Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) can authorize a 90-day experimental pilot in one educational zone using a simple administrative sanction.

QUERY 02

"Where does the capex come from for street screens and maintenance?"

The Strategy: Municipal corporations do not pay for the hardware. We co-locate with private outdoor advertising concessionaires who already hold long-term maintenance contracts for transit shelters. We upgrade their static slots to energy-efficient e-paper at zero cost to the municipality, funded by corporate hiring subscriptions.

QUERY 03

"How do you address public liability and data privacy on public screens?"

The Strategy: The physical display operates as a unidirectional broadcast screen that never collects or stores personal data. Commuter answers are submitted on personal mobile devices with air-gapped anonymization. All raw data is scrubbed and purged within 24 hours in full compliance with the DPDP Act 2023.

QUERY 04

"Why would transit authorities care about student employment?"

The Strategy: Transit authorities face significant pressure to demonstrate civic value beyond basic transportation. KOM converts unproductive dwell time (18.2 min daily average) into measurable skilling, directly contributing to the city's Smart Cities Mission annual assessment without requiring any municipal budget.
